By using Aspirants Guide, you agree to this Privacy Policy. If you do not agree, please do not use the app.

1. Information we collect

1.1 Information you give us

Aspirants Guide uses Google Sign-In. When you sign in, we receive from your Google account:

  • Your name
  • Your email address
  • Your profile picture
  • A unique Google account identifier

We do not receive your Google password at any time.

1.2 Information created by using the app

  • Exams you select (for example SSC, Railway, Banking)
  • Your subscription status and expiry date
  • Test attempts, scores, answers and bookmarked questions
  • Your app preferences, such as language

1.3 Information we do not collect

  • Card numbers, UPI IDs, or bank account details
  • Your location
  • Your contacts, photos, files, camera, or microphone
  • Your phone number

2. Why we use your information

WhatWhy

We do not use your information for advertising or marketing profiling.

3. Payments

Premium subscriptions are sold through Google Play Billing. When you subscribe:

  • All payment details are entered on Google's own payment screen, not ours.
  • We never see, receive, or store your card, UPI, or bank information.
  • We receive only a confirmation that a valid subscription exists, and its expiry date.

Payments are governed by the Google Privacy Policy and Google Play's terms. Refunds and cancellations are handled through Google Play.

5. Where your data is stored

Your account data is stored on Google Firebase servers located in India (asia-south1 region). Study content is delivered through Cloudflare's global network. Some service providers listed above may process limited data outside India as part of their normal operations.

6. How long we keep your data

We keep your account information for as long as your account is active. If you delete your account, your personal data is removed from our systems. Records that we are required to keep for tax or legal reasons, such as transaction records held by Google, may be retained for the period required by law.

7. Deleting your account and data

You can delete your account at any time:

  • Open the app, go to Profile → Delete Account, and confirm.
  • Or email us at aspirantsguideteam@gmail.com from your registered email address, with the subject "Delete my account".

Deleting your account removes your name, email, exam selections, and test history. Requests sent by email are completed within 30 days. Deleting your account does not automatically cancel your Google Play subscription — please cancel that separately in the Google Play Store to avoid further charges.

8. Security

We use industry-standard measures to protect your information, including encrypted connections (HTTPS), Google's secure authentication, and server-side rules that prevent users from modifying their own subscription status. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, but we take reasonable steps to protect your data.

9. Children

Aspirants Guide is a study application intended for users aged 18 and above who are preparing for government recruitment examinations. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, contact us and we will delete it.
